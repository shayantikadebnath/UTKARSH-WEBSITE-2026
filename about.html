About the Club

Where business students learn by building.

UTKARSH gives every member of the MHSS department a hand in shaping something real — turning ideas into initiatives, and classroom learning into lived experience.

Our Philosophy

Leadership isn’t taught in theory. It’s practiced.

From budgeting an event down to the last rupee, to crafting the branding that represents our department across campus — every role within UTKARSH is real responsibility with real impact. We believe the best managers aren’t made in lecture halls alone, but in the everyday work of building, leading, and showing up for a shared vision.
