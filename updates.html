All CRs are requested to share the Wall Magazine schedule in their respective official class groups and ensure that everyone goes through it carefully.

Please make sure that the students allocated to their respective PhD Scholar are present as per the schedule and establish contact with the concerned PhD Scholar. Enrollment No. 1 and 2 are specifically instructed to contact their respective PhD Scholar on Monday.

The Wall Magazine is an initiative taken by our HOD, Dr. Anirban Dutta Sir. All the details have already been communicated by Dr. Anuradha Nongmaithem Ma’am.
